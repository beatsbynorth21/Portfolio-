Bio

The sound

Northhh produces under the name Beats By Northhh — cinematic, dark, and melancholic instrumentals built entirely on a mobile phone, no studio, no laptop, just FL Studio Mobile and a clear sense of mood.

The catalog leans into tension and atmosphere: tracks like "Pray For Me," "Desert Smoke," "More Than A Phase," and "Bando" sit somewhere between trap and score work — built for artists who want a beat that already sounds like it means something before a single bar is written.
